Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Urban Store, we want customers to have a straightforward experience when purchasing our products.

1. Current Checkout Status

Please note that checkout is currently unavailable on this website.

This website is currently being used as a product presentation and demonstration website.

Therefore:

  • No orders are currently processed through the website
  • No payment is collected
  • No credit or debit card information is requested
  • Clicking the checkout button does not create an order
  • No refund is required for an order that has not been placed

2. Return Eligibility

For future purchases made through an active checkout service, eligible products may generally be returned within 30 days of delivery, subject to the applicable return requirements.

Returned products should generally be:

  • Unused
  • Unworn
  • Undamaged
  • In their original condition
  • Returned with original packaging where applicable

Certain items may not be eligible for return where applicable.

3. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a product that is damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible.

Please provide:

  • Your order information
  • A description of the issue
  • Photographs of the product where appropriate

We will review the issue and provide further instructions.

4. Return Process

Once our checkout and order-processing service becomes available, customers requesting an eligible return should contact our customer support team.

We may provide return instructions or a return address depending on the circumstances.

Customers should not send products back without receiving return instructions first.

5. Refunds

If a return is approved, the refund will generally be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase.

The time required for a refund to appear may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

6. Non-Returnable Items

Certain products may be excluded from returns where applicable, including products that:

  • Have been used or damaged after delivery
  • Are missing original packaging where required
  • Have been altered or modified
  • Cannot reasonably be resold due to their condition

Any applicable exclusions will be communicated at the time of purchase.

7. Sale or Promotional Items

Return eligibility for discounted or promotional products may vary depending on the applicable promotion and purchase terms.

Any special return conditions will be communicated before purchase.

8. Exchanges

If an exchange service is offered in the future, availability may depend on product stock and the condition of the returned item.

9. Changes to This Policy

Urban Store may update this Return & Refund Policy when our ordering, payment, delivery, or customer service processes change.

The latest version will always be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about returns, refunds, or product issues, please contact Urban Store through our Contact Us section.

Important: Since the current website checkout is unavailable, these return and refund terms are provided for future purchasing functionality and do not represent a current processed-order commitment.
