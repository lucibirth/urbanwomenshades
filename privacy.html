Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Urban Store, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you interact with our website, including:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Contact information
  • Information submitted through our Contact Us form
  • Messages or enquiries you send to us

We may also automatically receive limited technical information when you visit our website, such as browser type, device information, general usage information, and website interaction data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and requests
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our website and services
  • Maintain website security and functionality
  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Communicate with you when necessary regarding your enquiry

We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to the operation of our website without appropriate notice.

3. Cookies

Urban Store may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality and user experience.

Cookies may help us remember preferences, understand website usage, and maintain essential website functionality.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services, such as hosting providers, analytics services, content delivery networks, or external image providers.

These services may process certain technical information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through our website against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Information Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide requested services, resolve enquiries, maintain records, or comply with applicable requirements.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including requesting access, correction, or deletion of information you have provided.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact details provided on our website.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children who are unable to independently provide information or make purchases without appropriate supervision.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children for marketing purposes.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, or legal requirements.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Urban Store through our Contact Us section.
